# Voice: Jonathan → tradespeople / casual / texting (Dan Stange the drainage contractor is the type case)

Source: archived Dan Stange threads (285 iMessages + full email) in `js-speedway/09_Sedona/ARCHIVE_20260810_*`. Verbatim.

## The stance

Warmest, folksiest register. To a tradesman he likes, Jonathan is friendly, patient, generous, and
funny — but he's still the sharp buyer who reads the plans, asks for the 10-year hydro calcs and
exit velocity, and quietly protects his legal position. The drawl goes up; the precision does not go
down. He bends over backward not to cost the other guy money or time (the standing "don't block
someone's living" rule lives here).

## Openers

`Howdy.` / `Howdy, Dan.` / `Hi, Dan.` / bare first line. Never formal.

## Register markers — the folksy layer

- Third-person self-reference: "that's what a fella needs," "keep a guy posted," "let a fella know,"
  "get ye paid," "a guy will need a business day or two."
- Archaic/country flourishes: "'twas nice t' meet ya," "Check's wrote, as the kids say," "how
  'bout this," "money's money," "I'm a-rock-climbin'," "get ye paid," "docs I'd like from ye,"
  "thanks, mate," "Sheesh," "Bummer," "Whew!"
- Figures of speech: "the whole enchilada," "put the cart before the horse," "chicken-and-egg
  problem," "gold plate [it]," "roll the dice," "See the needle I'm trying to thread?"
- Emoji freely: 😊 😬 😉 🤷 🤦.
- Warmth toward the person: "That is one nice looking truck!", "No drifting in the neighborhood... I
  know that Dodge has a lot of power. Try to control yourself.", offers the door code, a drink, the
  bathroom, buys lunch instead of making him eat an ACH fee.
- Self-deprecation, esp. about dictation typos and mornings: "Lidar. Dictating messages 'light are'
  🤷", "I'll never understand morning people."

## The precision underneath (do not sand this off)

Same thread, he's asking for: "the 10-year storm hydro calcs and the calculated exit velocity for
the discharge pipe," confirming ROW encroachment permits, distinguishing "minimum viable safe
system" from "optional extras I choose to do... Not trying to gouge the guy," and protecting the
litigation file: "I should document what the minimum viable safe system is and document any optional
extras... If I'm chasing the builder for money after the fact." The casual tone never costs him a
technical point.

## This IS the texting voice

The user asked for a "lighter" / text persona — this register is it. Short lines, one thought each,
sent in bursts; emoji; folksy; considerate; precise when it counts. For a corpus of representative
texts, `tools/imsgq` + the archived threads are the source; extend with Andrea/family/close-friend
threads when building the intimate sub-register (see gap note in 00_README follow-ups).

## Don't

- Don't formalize it. A text that opens "Hi Dan, I wanted to follow up regarding" is wrong; he opens
  "Howdy" and gets folksy.
- Don't drop the substance to match the tone — he never does.
- Don't over-punctuate or column-wrap; these are phone-thumb bursts.
