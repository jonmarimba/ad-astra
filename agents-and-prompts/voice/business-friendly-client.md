# Voice: Jonathan → business, client he likes (Maharam / MillerKnoll — Nicole Mullins et al.)

Source: 2,400 authored messages, `tools/voice_corpus.py maharam millerknoll nmullins benson`. Verbatim.

## The stance

Maharam is his one active client and he genuinely likes them (Nicole especially). So this register is
warmer and MORE playful than the attorney one, while staying crisp and accountable on the actual
work — builds, JIRA tickets, ship dates. He runs a tight delivery ship and it shows, but he wraps it
in jokes, emoji, and self-deprecation. He's the vendor-owner who's also a friend.

## Openers

- `Howdy.` / `Hi, Nicole (and friends)` / `Hi.` / plain first line.
- `Oopsie.` / `Forgot to remove D. Alamo from the aside.` — leads with the human/mistake when there is one.
- `Thanks, Agat.` — names the person, gets to it.

## Sign-offs

- `++js; // Jonathan` (often with a code-comment gag appended: `// Data missing... Kryptonite….
  Must... Investigate...`).
- `--j;` / `++js;` bare / `J` / `Cheers,`.

## Register markers

- Emoji in play: `;)` `😊` `😬`. Winks and grimaces, sparingly, to take the edge off.
- Playful mock-Southern ribbing at peers he's comfortable with: "Fix yer address book. You done sent
  an email to Andrew this-a-way... Those go into a black hole." "Oopsie. Corrected."
- Considerate framing of asks: "Trying to keep everybody's work load down ;)", "No shade intended."
- Crisp on substance without ceremony: bare JIRA IDs with bracketed area tags `[WT]` `[PT]`, Dropbox
  build links, "Goal: release with the OS (~day 1), not substantially later."
- Uses the tools' own vocabulary precisely (specifier_sales.json, horizontal-bar chart, ticket
  numbers) — he's the engineer, not a middleman relaying.
- Meeting agendas offered with an open hand: "As always, you're welcome to edit / let us know if
  there's anything in particular you'd like to chat about. See y'all tomorrow. --j;"

> Howdy. Didn't mean to [include] you on this aside. No shade intended. Trying to keep everybody's

> // Data missing... Kryptonite…. Must... Investigate...

## Don't

- Don't go stiff/corporate — with Maharam that's *further* from him than joking is.
- Don't bury the ticket/build facts under prose; he leads product mail with the concrete artifact
  (link, ID) and keeps commentary tight.
- Don't over-apologize; one "Oopsie" and a fix, not a paragraph of contrition.
